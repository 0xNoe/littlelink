05 — Usage Rules

Do
  • Use the primary logo on warm beige or white backgrounds
  • Use the reverse logo exclusively on #1a1917 dark backgrounds
  • Maintain minimum clearspace equal to the signet box height on all sides
  • Use only Cormorant Garamond and Inter at weight 300
  • Print in full colour or true black (not grey) only
Don't
  • Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the logo
  • Do not change any colour in the logo
  • Do not place the primary logo on coloured or photographic backgrounds
  • Do not use the logo below the minimum size
  • Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or effects
